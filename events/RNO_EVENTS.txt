	id = RNO_Events_Lore.1
	title = "General Overview of The Newest Order History"
	desc = "The Republic and its history are a strange phenomenon — it simply appeared in the middle of the Atlantic Ocean, seemingly out of nowhere, in the year 1916. Its people already possessed a full understanding of the world, its history, and its cultures, as though they were native to it, and appeared entirely oblivious to the fact that their very existence defied all explanation.
	At the time of its arrival, the Republic was still known as the Kingdom of Poniatowski, and was quickly forced to choose a side in the Great War. It was thrown into the Entente, only to receive minimal recognition and meager reparations for its efforts. Following the war, a string of incompetent decisions by the Poniatowski crown eroded public trust to a breaking point, ultimately giving rise to a widely popular and broadly supported coup led by the allies of the reigning King. In its wake, power was — for the first time — shared with the people. The Crown still holds considerable authority, but it is no longer entirely unchecked.
	The young Republic now stands just one year from its first election since its founding, and with it, both its political future and its place in the world remain deeply uncertain. Some voices call for the restoration of a total monarchy under the new King, while others demand an end to the Crown entirely. But the monarchy is far from the only question haunting the country.
	As the nature of the Republic's mysterious appearance slowly becomes public knowledge, a burning desire has emerged to understand — and perhaps harness — the power behind its magical origins. Some have gone further still, seeing themselves as members of a superior race by virtue of it. The Republic stands at a crossroads on many fronts, and where it goes from here remains to be seen.
